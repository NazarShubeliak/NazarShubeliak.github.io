title: Online tabletop RPG platform
summary: A virtual tabletop for D&D with fog of war, tokens, music and a campaign wiki.
problem: Playing D&D online with friends needed one place for characters, maps and campaign lore.
solution: A web app where players create characters through an interactive sheet and join the game master's room by code. The table supports maps, player and monster tokens, fog of war, walls and music. A lore editor works like a campaign wiki, and the game master decides what players can see. A mini-game in the lobby keeps players busy while they wait.
result: Every action on the table appears for all players in real time; we use it for our own games.
