title: Detective board for investigation games
summary: An atmospheric Call of Cthulhu platform built around a drag-and-drop investigation board.
problem: Investigation games need a shared place to lay out clues and connect them, which a regular game map can't give.
solution: 'Each player has a private board and the group shares a common one: documents, photos and case files can be dragged, pinned and connected with red string, like a real investigation wall. Players create characters through an interactive sheet, and the game master keeps hidden lore and controls the music.'
result: A complex drag-and-drop interface, close to whiteboard tools like Miro, synced between players in real time.
