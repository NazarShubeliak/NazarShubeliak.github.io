title: Automated ad reporting
summary: Data from the ad platforms collected on schedule and turned into weekly and monthly reports.
problem: An accountant copied the numbers from every ad account into a spreadsheet by hand, which took a lot of time and invited mistakes.
solution: Scheduled Make.com automations collect data from Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, Microsoft Advertising and invoices from GetMyInvoices. Google Apps Script turns the raw data into weekly and monthly reports in Google Sheets. Each source runs as its own scenario, so one failing source never stops the rest.
result: Reports that used to be assembled by hand now build themselves.
